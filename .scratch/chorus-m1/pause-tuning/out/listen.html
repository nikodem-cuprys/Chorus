Setting and heard silence differ. Each player shows the setting and the silence actually heard there (quieter than −50 dB). Between lines, the heard silence is the setting plus the 0.08 s kept at the two trimmed ends. Between sentences, inside one take, it is much longer: voicing joins the sentences as the voice model wrote them, each with its own silence at the end and start, which is not trimmed. If sentence pauses sound too long even at the shortest setting, that is the cause, and it can be fixed in voicing.
