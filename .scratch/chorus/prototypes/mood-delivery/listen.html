Jane Eyre, chapter 1: John Reed finds Jane (dialogue-heavy)

The Scene's lines

John Reed (angry, normal): Boh! Madam Mope!

Narrator: cried the voice of John Reed; then he paused: he found the room apparently empty.

John Reed (angry, normal): Where the dickens is she!

Narrator: he continued.

John Reed (angry, strong): Lizzy! Georgy! Joan is not here: tell mama she is run out into the rain, bad animal!

Jane (anxious, normal): It is well I drew the curtain,

Narrator: thought I; and I wished fervently he might not discover my hiding-place: nor would John Reed have found it out himself; he was not quick either of vision or conception;

Narrator: but Eliza just put her head in at the door, and said at once:

Eliza Reed (neutral, normal): She is in the window-seat, to be sure, Jack.

Narrator: And I came out immediately, for I trembled at the idea of being dragged forth by the said Jack.

Jane (anxious, normal): What do you want?

Narrator: I asked, with awkward diffidence.

John Reed (neutral, normal): Say, What do you want, Master Reed?

Narrator: was the answer.

John Reed (neutral, normal): I want you to come here;

Narrator: and seating himself in an arm-chair, he intimated by a gesture that I was to approach and stand before him.

Metamorphosis, opening: Gregor wakes (narration-heavy)

The Scene's lines

Narrator: One morning, when Gregor Samsa woke from troubled dreams, he found himself transformed in his bed into a horrible vermin.

Narrator: He lay on his armour-like back, and if he lifted his head a little he could see his brown belly, slightly domed and divided by arches into stiff sections.

Narrator: The bedding was hardly able to cover it and seemed ready to slide off any moment.

Narrator: His many legs, pitifully thin compared with the size of the rest of him, waved about helplessly as he looked.

Gregor (anxious, strong): What's happened to me?

Narrator: he thought. It wasn't a dream.

Narrator: His room, a proper human room although a little too small, lay peacefully between its four familiar walls.

Narrator: Gregor then turned to look out the window at the dull weather.

Narrator: Drops of rain could be heard hitting the pane, which made him feel quite sad.

Gregor (sad, normal): How about if I sleep a little bit longer and forget all this nonsense,

Narrator: he thought, but that was something he was unable to do because he was used to sleeping on his right, and in his present state couldn't get into that position.

Narrator: However hard he threw himself onto his right, he always rolled back to where he was.
